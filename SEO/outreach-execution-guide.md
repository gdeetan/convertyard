**Time:** ~6 hours total
**Copy to use:** `SEO/outreach-copy.md`

### Rules before posting
- Read each subreddit's rules before posting
- Do not post the same message in multiple subreddits on the same day
- Add genuine value — answer existing questions, don't just drop a link
- One subreddit per day max

## Phase 4 — Blog Outreach
**When:** Month 3+, after you have 15+ referring domains
**Time:** 10–15 outreach emails per week (~1.5 hrs/week)

### Before sending any pitch
- [ ] You are live on at least 15 directories
- [ ] You have real traffic (show in Google Search Console)
- [ ] The pitch in the xlsx has been read and sounds like you — edit anything that doesn't

### How to send
1. Open `SEO/outreach-targets.xlsx` → Sheet 2
2. Filter by Priority = High, Status = blank
3. Copy the pitch from the Pitches column
4. Find the author's name from the referenced article — personalize "Hi [name]"
5. Send from your personal email (gdtwebmaster@gmail.com or a branded one if you have it)
6. Mark status in the xlsx after sending
7. Follow up once after 10 days if no reply — one follow-up only

### Priority order for outreach
Send in this order:

**Week 1 of outreach:** PDF Tools — High Priority
- How-To Geek, MakeUseOf, Lifehacker, Zapier Blog, TechRadar

**Week 2:** Image Tools — High Priority
- PetaPixel, Fstoppers, 9to5Mac, Smashing Magazine

**Week 3:** Developer — High Priority
- CSS-Tricks (DigitalOcean), DZone, Web Tools Weekly, JavaScript Weekly, TLDR Tech

**Week 4:** OCR + AI
- Lifehacker (OCR angle), Zapier Blog (OCR angle), Ben's Bites, The Rundown AI, TLDR AI

**Month 4+:** Medium priority targets, podcasts, general newsletters

## Tracking

Update the Status column in `SEO/outreach-targets.xlsx` after every action:
- `Submitted` — directory or form submission done
- `Sent` — pitch email sent (add date)
- `Following up` — no reply after 10 days, sent one follow-up
- `Live` — link confirmed live
- `No response` — after follow-up, no reply

Check monthly in Google Search Console:
- Referring domains (target: 15+ by Month 1, 50+ by Month 3)
- Organic impressions (target: 500+ by Month 1, 5,000+ by Month 3)
- Organic clicks (target: 50+ by Month 1, 500+ by Month 3)
