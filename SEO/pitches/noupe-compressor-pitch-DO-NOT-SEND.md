## Broader pattern reinforcement — stop researching this category

20 pitches researched. The high-value five have been identified for many rounds now. Every additional pitch in the "big design/dev blog with a 5-8 year old tool review" category has fallen into the skip tier. Categories that consistently fail:

- **CDN/image-hosting product blogs** (KeyCDN, ImageKit, Cloudinary, imgix, Bunny.net) — funnel hostility
- **Big SaaS content-marketing blogs** (Buffer, Sprout, Shopify, HubSpot, WP Engine) — no contributor channel
- **Editorially inconsistent publications with dead submission paths** (ImprovePhotography, Noupe) — nothing to submit to
- **Paid-link operations** (ExpertPhotography) — pay-to-play, not editorial

**Actionable:** Do not research any more pitches in these categories. If the target isn't (a) a publication with a real editorial submission process, (b) a fresh article with an active author, or (c) an author-acknowledged gap you can slot into — it's a skip. Save the research hours for either executing the five high-value pitches you already have, or self-publishing on convertyard.com / Hashnode.
