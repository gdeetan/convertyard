**Tool:** ConvertYard — https://convertyard.com
**What it is:** browser-based batch image compression and format conversion (HEIC / JPG / PNG / WebP / AVIF), running entirely in the browser via WebAssembly. Nothing is uploaded to a server — the conversion happens in the reader's own browser tab.

**Where it fits vs. JPEGmini:**
Hillary opened her JPEGmini review talking about rural internet and being controlled by hotspot Wi-Fi when she travels — that moment when you need to compress a folder of client photos before uploading them from wherever you are. That's the workflow ConvertYard was built for. It's not a Lightroom plug-in replacement — for the sit-down editing session with all your gear, JPEGmini's tighter Lightroom integration is a real advantage and Hillary rated it correctly at 5/5.

Where ConvertYard is different:
- Works on any device with a browser, including an iPad in an airport or a friend's laptop
- No install, no plug-in registration, no license transfer between machines
- Handles HEIC input directly (iPhone or newer mirrorless — the case where JPEGmini's workflow assumes you've already exported to JPG)
- Free, no subscription and no upfront cost — different price point from JPEGmini's $59/$89
